Notes for reviewers: what is real and what is a placeholder
  • Disease result: scripted. It depends only on the crop picked: wheat gives yellow rust, cotton gives leaf curl virus, mango gives powdery mildew. The real app will use an on-phone model, tested on Multan field photos.
  • Voice: the page reads each screen aloud with the phone's Urdu voice, if it has one. There is no Saraiki voice yet. The real app will play recordings by native Saraiki and Punjabi speakers.
  • Saraiki and Punjabi text: first drafts, still to be checked by native speakers.
  • Advice, prices and weather: sample safety steps and sample numbers, not real advice or market rates.
  • Airplane mode: the switch at the top shows how the app behaves with no internet. Open the page once online, then turn on real airplane mode and keep using it without reloading.
